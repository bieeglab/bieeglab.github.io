Biography

Maya Wong is a joint postdoctoral fellow with the Brain Imaging and EEG Laboratory at UCSF and the San Francisco VA and Holly Hamilton’s Clinical Cognitive Psychophysiology Lab at the University of Minnesota. Her research interests include psychosis, minority stress, recovery, resilience, and the biosocial determinants of mental health.

Public VA and UCSF listings describe her work in clinical research and early psychosis, including assessment and therapy roles with the Path Program. This profile brings together those professional sources with her new lab listing.
