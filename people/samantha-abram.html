Biography

Samantha Abram is a clinical psychologist whose research examines motivation, decision-making, and goal-directed behavior in people with psychotic-spectrum disorders. She combines behavioral tasks and neuroscience methods to study why initiating and sustaining goals can be difficult in severe mental illness.

Her research includes EEG studies of reward processing and memory in schizophrenia, as well as work on sensory prediction and brain connectivity. She earned her PhD in Clinical Psychology at the University of Minnesota and completed clinical internship and advanced fellowship training at the San Francisco VA and UCSF.
