Biography

Maria Kryza-Lacombe studies the intersection of emotional and cognitive health, drawing on neuroscience, neuropsychology, and clinical psychology. Her work focuses on reward processing, positive affect, well-being, and the ways these processes change with aging and depression.

She uses complementary EEG and fMRI methods to investigate neural mechanisms linking depression and cognitive decline in older adults. Her broader goal is to inform interventions that support mental and cognitive health in aging. She completed doctoral training in Clinical Psychology through the SDSU/UC San Diego joint program and neuropsychology/geropsychology fellowship training at the San Francisco VA.
