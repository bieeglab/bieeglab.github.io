Biography

Michael Jacob is a psychiatrist and neuroscientist investigating how electrical brain activity relates to blood-flow and metabolic signals in schizophrenia. His work uses simultaneous EEG and fMRI, alongside cardiovascular measurements, to study communication between the heart and brain.

His research interests include neurometabolic coupling, embodied cognition, and the psychobiology of psychotherapy. He trained in neurobiology and anatomy and medicine at the University of Rochester, followed by psychiatry residency at UCSF and psychiatric research fellowship training at the San Francisco VA.
