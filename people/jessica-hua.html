Biography

Jessica Hua studies the mechanisms underlying schizophrenia and the development and persistence of psychosis. Her research combines neuroimaging, behavioral measures, and physiological recordings across the illness course, from clinical high risk to more established illness.

Her interests include EEG, MRI, neural plasticity, and brain stimulation. Her long-term research goals include improving prediction of psychosis risk and informing interventions grounded in an understanding of brain mechanisms. She earned her PhD in Clinical Psychology at the University of Missouri and completed postdoctoral training at the San Francisco VA and UCSF.
