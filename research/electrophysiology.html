Research areas

Electrophysiology

EEG captures the timing of brain activity. Our research examines responses to sounds and auditory oddball tasks, as well as brain signals during decision-making and gambling tasks. Simultaneous EEG and fMRI recordings connect the timing of electrical activity with patterns of brain function.
